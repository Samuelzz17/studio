# **App Name**: SR

## Core Features:

- Menu Management: Add, edit, and manage menu items with descriptions, prices, and images.
- Inventory Tracking: Track the current stock levels of each menu item's ingredients, including automated low-stock alerts.
- Point of Sale (POS) Interface: Simple and intuitive interface for processing customer orders.
- Sales Tracking: Record each transaction with timestamps, total cost, items sold and payment methods.
- Daily Sales Summaries: Generate summaries of daily sales, including total revenue, most popular items, and peak hours. Automatically email a daily sales report to the owner at the end of the day.
- Financial Reporting: Provide basic financial reports, including income statements and expense tracking based on inventory usage and other inputs.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm brown (#A67B5B) to evoke the feeling of coffee and earth.
- Background color: Cream (#F5F5DC), a very light tint of the primary hue, creates a neutral and inviting canvas.
- Accent color: Soft green (#8FBC8F), analogous to brown, suggesting freshness and a natural touch. Slightly brighter and more saturated than the brown and cream, for appropriate contrast.
- Font pairing: 'Belleza' for headlines (sans-serif), giving the site a touch of style, art, and design, paired with 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text.
- Use simple, hand-drawn style icons to represent menu categories, ingredients, and other actions within the app.
- A clean and spacious layout that prioritizes user experience with intuitive navigation and a focus on readability.
- Subtle animations and transitions when adding items to the POS, confirming orders, or viewing sales summaries to give users clear feedback.
